Reference DB

/reference/

Curated plant virus reference sequences from NCBI GenBank, cross-referenced with ICTV VMR MSL41. 12,000+ curated reference sequences, 4,800+ species, 7-step QC pipeline.

🌿

Symptom Photos

/photos/

2,854 curated symptom photos from EPPO, DPV, books and research papers (DOI-linked, AI-screened). Filter by host, bilingual symptom, and photo position.

→
🔗

Virus–Vector–Host Relations

/relations/

All four relationship views in one place: host–virus association Sankey, virus–host and vector–virus–host networks, and literature-backed vector–host tables. 14,791 virus–host pairs from 199,070 GenBank accessions, plus 1,806 vector records.

→
🌐

Plant EVE Landscape

/eve/

Endogenous viral elements across 1,527 plant genomes and 284 host families — 150,136 loci. Eight linked views: virus family, host family, DNA/RNA type, family×host matrix, ancient infection, amplification dynamics, RT phylogeny and QC.

→
🧬

Resistance Genes & sRNAs

/genes/

RefPlantNLR 415 NLRs + 11,780 sRNA genes + 3,996 UPS + 83,166 miRNAs. Integrated plant immune gene atlas.

→
🧪

Primer Database

/primers/

103,642 designed PCR/qPCR primer pairs covering 4,789 plant virus species. Designed with Primer3, varVAMP and Olivar (tiling).

→
🌱

1KP Virome

/onekp/

Virus detection across 1,341 plant transcriptomes. 96 virus species (118 high-confidence detections), interactive coverage and prevalence analysis.

→
🧊

Protein Structure

/structures3d/

AlphaFold2-predicted 3D structures of plant virus proteins — 17,918 full-atom models across 1,832 species, with domain annotation. Browse by species and protein, then view the model in the Molstar viewer.

Virus Explorer

/explorer/

Interactive Dash-based explorer: 5D linked filtering, timeline, global map, genome viewer, taxonomy tree, MSA viewer — click through to each virus's full profile (taxonomy, genome plots, hosts, vectors, primers, photos).

Metabuli

/metabuli/

Upload contigs for plant virus metagenomic classification. 8-rank taxonomy, BLASTN/BLASTX/CDD validation, Krona/Sankey viz.

PV-miRNA

/pamirdb/

Plant virus miRNA target prediction. 3-algorithm consensus (miRanda + psRNATarget + RNAhybrid), 402 plants x 42 viruses.

🌲

Virus Phylogeny

/phylo/ · /phylo/molecular/

Two complementary views of plant virus evolution: the ICTV MSL41 taxonomy tree (set nesting, no branch lengths) and official molecular trees from ICTV Report chapters (maximum-likelihood, real branch lengths and support values).
