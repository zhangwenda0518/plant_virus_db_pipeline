Reference DB

/reference/

Curated plant virus reference sequences from NCBI GenBank, cross-referenced with ICTV VMR MSL41. 9,022 curated reference sequences, 5,147 species, 7-step QC pipeline.

Symptom Photos

/photos/

2,975 curated symptom photos from EPPO, DPV, books and research papers (DOI-linked, AI-screened). Filter by host, bilingual symptom, and photo position.

→

Virus–Vector–Host Relations

/relations/

All four relationship views in one place: host–virus association Sankey, virus–host and vector–virus–host networks, and literature-backed vector–host tables. 15,197 virus–host pairs from 211,132 GenBank accessions, plus 1,803 vector records.

→
🌐

Plant EVE Landscape

/eve/

Endogenous viral elements across 1,527 plant genomes and 284 host families — 150,136 loci. Eight linked views: virus family, host family, DNA/RNA type, family×host matrix, ancient infection, amplification dynamics, RT phylogeny and QC.

→
🧬

Resistance Genes & sRNAs

/genes/

RefPlantNLR 415 NLRs + 11,780 sRNA genes + 3,996 UPS + 83,166 miRNAs. Integrated plant immune gene atlas.

→
🧪

Primer Database

/primers/

83,830 unique PCR/qPCR primer pairs (deduplicated from 103,642 designs) covering 4,789 plant virus species, with 66,891 rated RECOMMENDED. Designed with Primer3, varVAMP and Olivar (tiling).

→
🌱

1KP Virome

/onekp/

Virus detection across 1,341 plant transcriptomes. 96 virus species (118 high-confidence detections), interactive coverage and prevalence analysis.

→
🧊

Protein Structure

/structures3d/

53,000+ structures across 4,200+ species — RVPSD AF2, VAD, UniProt PDB/AFDB structures, our AlphaFold3 pipeline, Modeller homology and self-fold models, with source filters, coverage atlas and a Molstar viewer.

Virus Explorer

/explorer/

Interactive Dash-based explorer: 5D linked filtering, timeline, global map, genome viewer, taxonomy tree, MSA viewer — click through to each virus's full profile (taxonomy, genome plots, hosts, vectors, primers, photos).

→
🧫

Metabuli

/metabuli/

Upload contigs for plant virus metagenomic classification. 8-rank taxonomy, BLASTN/BLASTX/CDD validation, Krona/Sankey viz.

→
🎯

PV-miRNA

/pamirdb/

Plant virus miRNA target prediction. Custom Smith-Waterman alignment engine with local heuristic recheck layers (no external tools), 11,006 pre-computed predictions across 799 plant species and 40 virus families.

→
🌲

Virus Phylogeny

/phylo/（one page · two tabs）

Two complementary views on one page (tab switcher): the ICTV MSL41 taxonomy tree (set nesting, no branch lengths) and official molecular trees from ICTV Report chapters (maximum-likelihood, real branch lengths and support values).
